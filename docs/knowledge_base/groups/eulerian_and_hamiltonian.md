## Central idea
This group contrasts edge-covering traversal problems with vertex-covering traversal problems.

## Main dependencies
- depends on traversal language
- Eulerian themes connect strongly to degree conditions
- Hamiltonian themes connect strongly to extremal structure and tournaments
