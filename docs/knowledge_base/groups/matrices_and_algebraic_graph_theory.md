## Central idea
This group turns graph structure into algebraic objects, making counting, connectivity, and structural analysis accessible through matrices and polynomials.

## Main dependencies
- depends on foundations and trees
- feeds into spanning-tree counting
- supports global structure reasoning from local adjacency data
