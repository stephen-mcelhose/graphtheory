# Advanced Exercises

1. Explain why every tournament has a Hamiltonian path.
2. Use a de Bruijn digraph example to explain why balanced indegree/outdegree matters for Eulerian circuits.
3. Compare Eulerian and Hamiltonian conditions on small named graphs.
4. Explore a graph that satisfies Dirac's condition and test Hamiltonian search.
5. Compute the chromatic polynomial of a small graph and interpret the coefficients.
