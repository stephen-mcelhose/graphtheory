# Chapter 10 Summary: More About Paths

## Core topics
- Menger's theorems
- arc-disjoint, edge-disjoint, and vertex-disjoint path systems
- separators and cuts
- Gallai–Milgram theorem
- path-missing sets
- Elser's sums

## Main ideas
- Path robustness can be measured by disjoint path multiplicity.
- Menger-style results equate maximum independent-path families with minimum separating bottlenecks.
- Gallai–Milgram studies covering directed graphs by paths.

## Why this chapter matters
This chapter is a culmination of traversal, cut, and optimization ideas.
