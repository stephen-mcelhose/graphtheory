# Chapter 8 Summary: Matchings

## Core topics
- definition of matchings
- matched vertices and partners
- perfect and A-complete matchings
- bipartite graphs
- Hall's marriage theorem
- König and Hall–König results
- systems of representatives
- regular bipartite graphs
- Latin squares
- magic matrices
- Birkhoff–von Neumann theorem

## Main ideas
- Matchings are edge sets with no shared endpoints.
- Bipartite structure makes matchings especially tractable.
- Hall's theorem is a central bridge between set systems and graph matchings.
- Matching theory has broad combinatorial applications.

## Why this chapter matters
It is one of the most algorithmically useful chapters and is foundational for the project's matching/flow bridge.

## Learning connection
