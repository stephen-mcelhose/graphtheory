# Chapter 6 Summary: Colorings

## Core topics
- proper colorings
- 2-colorings in undirected and directed settings
- Brooks theorems
- chromatic polynomial
- Vizing's theorem
- recent coloring results

## Main ideas
- Coloring treats adjacency as a conflict relation.
- Bipartiteness is the simplest nontrivial coloring phenomenon.
- Counting colorings leads to graph polynomials.
- Coloring mixes constructive algorithms with harder structural theorems.

## Why this chapter matters
It introduces one of the central invariant families in graph theory and links structure to counting.
