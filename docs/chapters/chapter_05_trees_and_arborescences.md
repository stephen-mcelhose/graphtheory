## Main ideas
- Trees are minimal connected graphs and maximal acyclic graphs.
- Directed analogues lead to arborescences.
- Counting spanning structures connects graph theory to determinants and Laplacians.
- de Bruijn sequences emerge from Eulerian reasoning on directed overlap graphs.

## Why this chapter matters
This is one of the richest chapters in the paper and connects structure, algorithms, and algebraic counting.
