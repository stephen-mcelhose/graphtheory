# Chapter 3 Summary: Multigraphs

## Core topics
- definition of multigraphs
- conversion from simple graphs to multigraphs
- parallel edges and loops
- generalization of prior concepts from simple graphs
- Eulerian circuits and walks
- Euler–Hierholzer theorem

## Main ideas
- Multigraphs generalize simple graphs by allowing multiple edges and loop-like edge behavior.
- Many simple-graph notions survive but require multiplicity-aware reinterpretation.
- Eulerian reasoning becomes especially natural in multigraphs.

## Why this chapter matters
This chapter motivates richer edge models and supports Eulerian traversal theory.

## Learning connection
It feeds naturally into `graph/multigraph.go`, Eulerian docs, and constructive circuit algorithms.
