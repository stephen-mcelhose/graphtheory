# Chapter 4 Summary: Digraphs and Multidigraphs

## Core topics
- simple digraphs and multidigraphs
- sources, targets, indegrees, outdegrees
- subdigraphs and conversions
- walks, paths, cycles in directed settings
- adjacency matrix
- strong and weak connectivity
- sink components
- Eulerian and Hamiltonian structure in digraphs
- tournaments
- Rédei theorems
- Hamiltonian cycles in tournaments
- Vandermonde-related application

## Main ideas
- Directed edges model asymmetric relations and one-way traversal.
- Reachability splits into strong and weak notions.
- Tournaments form a rich named class with strong Hamiltonian properties.
- Matrix representations begin to play a central role.

## Why this chapter matters
It provides the conceptual basis for directed traversal, SCCs, tournaments, and adjacency-matrix viewpoints.
