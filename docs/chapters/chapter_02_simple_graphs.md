# Chapter 2 Summary: Simple Graphs

## Core topics
- definition of a simple graph
- adjacency, neighbors, edges, vertices
- graph drawings
- Ramsey number #$#R(3,3)=6#$#
- degrees and the handshake lemma
- Mantel's theorem
- graph isomorphism
- named graph families: complete, empty, path, cycle, Kneser, hypercube, subset graphs, Cartesian products
- subgraphs and disjoint unions
- walks, paths, cycles, connectedness, components
- bridges and dominating sets
- Hamiltonian paths and cycles
- Ore and Dirac criteria

## Main ideas
- Simple graphs encode pairwise symmetric relations.
- Local quantities such as degree support global structural reasoning.
- Walks and paths provide the language of reachability and connectedness.
- Named graph families serve as canonical examples.
- Hamiltonian structure is introduced as a vertex-covering traversal problem.

## Why this chapter matters
This is the foundational chapter for the entire project. Much of the Go library's graph, traversal, and named-graph functionality comes from this chapter.

## Learning connection
This chapter should feed directly into `docs/named_graphs/`, `graph/`, `generators/`, and `traversal/`.
